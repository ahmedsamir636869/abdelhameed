“

What began in 1988 as a family-owned business in a 1,400 m² facility has grown into a modern manufacturing operation spanning 10,000 m² in 10th of Ramadan City. For more than three decades, ABD-ELHAMID has evolved with one constant:

a commitment to getting better.

Today, with a production capacity exceeding 4,000 tons annually and a team of more than 200 qualified professionals, the company is recognized as a market leader in Egypt and across the Middle East in the manufacture of wire components for household appliances, retail, supermarkets, warehouse pallet rack wire mesh decking, and diverse industrial applications.

Built on quality, precision, innovation, and reliability, ABD-ELHAMID continues to serve leading manufacturers across Egypt and neighboring markets.
